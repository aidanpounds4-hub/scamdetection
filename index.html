Scammers work by creating pressure so you act before you think. Paste the message, email, or a note about the call below and PauseFirst will point out the warning signs.

If in doubt, pause and verify

  • Don't click links or call numbers from the message. Look up the real number yourself.
  • No legitimate organization asks for your password, full card number, or one-time code.
  • Urgency and secrecy are pressure tactics. Real institutions let you take your time.
  • Talk to someone you trust before sending money, gift cards, or crypto.
